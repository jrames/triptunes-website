Spotify and more

Take your mixtape with you

Trip Tunes still saves straight into Apple Music. Now a finished tape can also go to Spotify and other supported music services: tap Export, pick where it’s headed, and Trip Tunes hands the song list to Soundiiz to finish the transfer.

Always use the same service? Check Use this every time and skip the question next trip.

Transfers to other services are provided by Soundiiz. Destination services may require an account or subscription, and track availability can vary. Trip Tunes never sees your passwords or sign-ins for those services.

Unlimited Tapes

Pay once. Stop counting tapes.

Every new mix still starts with a blank tape. Now there’s a tape that never runs out: Unlimited Tapes is a one-time purchase that lets you keep making mixes without spending your blank tapes, with no Trip Tunes subscription.

Prefer to top up as you go? A 10-tape refill is still on the shelf. Any tapes you already have stay yours when you upgrade, and your purchase restores on a new iPhone.

Unlimited Tapes is for personal use; fair-use limits help prevent abuse.

Your library, everywhere

Your tapes, together.

Your saved Trip Tunes library syncs automatically through your own iCloud account. Reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone, and your synced tapes return when you sign in with the same iCloud account. Your collection stays in step across your iPhones.

Cleaning out the case? Slide a tape sideways and trash it. Change your mind, and Undo puts it right back. Copies you’ve already exported to a music service stay where they are.

Requires an iCloud account with available storage. Library sync uses your private iCloud storage. Tapes you choose to share are handled separately.
