The Seattle Grunge Road Trip

Point the car toward I-5 and let Seattle's whole family tree ride along. This tape opens with the names the city gave the world — Nirvana's Drain You, Pearl Jam's State of Love and Trust, Soundgarden's Fell On Black Days, Jimi Hendrix's The Wind Cries Mary — then digs into the deep cuts: The Sonics out of Tacoma, Death Cab for Cutie's Bellingham beginnings, Sir Mix-A-Lot's Posse On Broadway, Shabazz Palaces and Blue Scholars repping the Central District.

The real find is what's underneath: this tape leans hard on artists who are genuinely from the towns along this route — some as well-known as Fleet Foxes and The Head and the Heart, others as under-the-radar as Zeffon, Dale Crover, and TAD. Riff-heavy, homegrown, and built for the drive up the coast.
