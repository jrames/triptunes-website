Gift Tape · Dolly Parton

Dolly’s Tennessee Long Way Home

Start in Nashville, point the hood toward the Smokies, and let Dolly Parton ride shotgun. This tape follows the Tennessee thread through Down on Music Row, My Tennessee Mountain Home, Tennessee Homesick Blues, and Coat of Many Colors, with enough 9 to 5, Jolene, and Two Doors Down energy to keep the windows open.

It’s a long way home, but the company is excellent: Dolly’s Light of a Clear Blue Morning, Kenny Rogers’ The Gambler, and their unmistakable Islands In the Stream. A road-trip tribute to Tennessee’s greatest storyteller—warm, funny, homesick, and ready for the next exit.
